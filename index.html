§ 01 · The Mission

Mountains are the metaphor. Community is the mission.

I.

Climb hard.

We take on the world's most demanding peaks — Denali, Ama Dablam, Aconcagua, Everest, Shasta — as an act of solidarity with those facing cancer.

II.

Raise funds.

Every step is sponsored. Every summit a fundraiser. 100% of donations flow to the Cancer Support Community South Bay.

III.

Build bridges.

Through the USA–Nepal Friendship Team we unite American and Nepali mountaineers — proving the global climbing community moves as one.

"Cancer is the crux. We don't surrender to the crux —
we keep climbing." — Field note · Base camp
