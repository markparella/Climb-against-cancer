Expeditions.

Every climb is a chapter. Every summit a paragraph for someone fighting their own crux. These are the expeditions that built this campaign.

§ 01 · Complete Record

Six peaks. Three continents. One mission.

2023
Denali · Round Two Alaska, USA · 20,310 ft
For the second year in a row, Climb Against Cancer and the US–Nepal Friendship Team returned to the highest mountain in North America.
→
2022
Denali · Friendship Team Alaska, USA · 20,310 ft
Inaugural seven-climber USA–Nepal Friendship Team summits June 8 — diamond jubilee of Whittaker's 1963 Everest.
→
2021
Ama Dablam Khumbu, Nepal · 22,349 ft
A technical Himalayan climb in the shadow of Everest. The proving ground that seeded the Friendship Team.
→
2020
Mt. Shasta California, USA · 14,179 ft
A pandemic-era benefit climb of California's volcanic giant. The world paused; the mountain did not.
→
—
Aconcagua Mendoza, Argentina · 22,838 ft
The highest peak in the Western Hemisphere. A foundational summit and original training ground for the campaign.
→
—
Everest Nepal · Tibet · 29,032 ft
The roof of the world — the reason a Denver Navy veteran ended up partnering with Nepali climbers for a cancer fundraiser.
→
