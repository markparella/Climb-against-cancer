// THE MISSION Three pillars. One summit.

Climb. Hard.

We tackle the world's most demanding peaks — Denali, Ama Dablam, Aconcagua, Everest, Shasta — as a deliberate act of solidarity with those facing the hardest climb of their lives.

Raise. Funds.

Every step is sponsored. Every summit is a fundraiser. 100% of donations flow to the Cancer Support Community South Bay so that no patient or family faces cancer alone.

Build. Bridges.

Through the USA–Nepal Friendship Team we unite American and Nepali mountaineers — proving that the global climbing community moves as one.
