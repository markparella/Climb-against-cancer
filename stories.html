2023

Denali · Round Two
Alaska, USA

20,310 FTJUNE 6USA × NEPAL

On June 6th, 2023 — for the second year in a row — Climb Against Cancer and the US–Nepal Friendship Team set out for the highest mountain in North America. The mission: another fundraiser for the Cancer Support Community South Bay; another reminder that this work doesn't stop after one trip.

2022

Denali · Friendship Team
Alaska, USA

20,310 FTJUNE 87 CLIMBERS

Mark's third attempt on the mountain became Climb Against Cancer's first complete summit. Seven climbers — the inaugural USA–Nepal Friendship Team — topped out together on June 8, 2022, marking the diamond jubilee of Jim Whittaker's first American summit of Everest, May 1, 1963.

2021

Ama Dablam
Khumbu, Nepal

22,349 FTHIMALAYA

A technical, exposed climb in the shadow of Everest. Ama Dablam is the peak the Nepalese call the "Mother's Necklace" — and the proving ground where the seed of the USA–Nepal Friendship Team was planted.
