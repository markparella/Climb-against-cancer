About.

Climb Against Cancer was founded in 2016 by Mark Parella — a US Navy veteran now based in Denver, Colorado — as a benefit mountaineering project. Our work is simple: climb hard, raise funds, deliver them where they matter.

FILE 02 DOC // CAC-002

Mark
Parella.

Founder · Lead climber · US Navy veteran

Born on the East Coast and forged at sea, Mark Parella spent his Navy years learning that hard things are done one foot in front of the other. He took that lesson into the mountains — and in 2016, after watching cancer touch too many of the people in his life, he turned it into a campaign.

Climb Against Cancer was the answer: a personal vow to use every expedition to raise money and visibility for the Cancer Support Community South Bay. From Mt. Shasta to Ama Dablam to three attempts on Denali, every climb is dedicated to someone fighting their own crux.

"Bringing happiness and self-worth by making a difference in someone else's life" — that is the entire brief.

75 Years.

The Friendship Team was assembled to honor 75 years of official diplomatic relations between the United States and Nepal — two nations bound by the mountains.

07 Climbers.

Seven of the finest American and Nepali mountaineers stood on the summit of Denali on June 8, 2022 — the team's first complete ascent under the Climb Against Cancer banner.

01 Mission.

One unified objective: raise money and awareness for cancer patients and their families. Every rope team carries the same flag.
